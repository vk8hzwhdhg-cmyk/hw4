# Campus Customs: Design Changes (Problem 10)

| Change | What changed | Why it keeps customers around and buying |
|---|---|---|
| **Matches the official store** | Open Sans type; uppercase headings; white header; Yale-blue (#00366b) buttons; cream footer. All taken from yalebulldogblue.com's theme. | Shoppers recognize the official look right away and trust it enough to buy. |
| **Lifestyle banner on Home** | The real site's photo of students wearing Yale tees and sweatshirts, shown whole, with the headline and buttons below. Navy background if the image is missing. | People buy apparel they can picture themselves in. A warm first impression keeps visitors scrolling into the products. |
| **"Boola Boola" discount** | Logged-in shoppers who cheer "Boola Boola" to Bulldog get a code (Boola####): 10% off, 7 days, once a year. | The reward encourages sign-ups and return visits. The 7-day expiry creates urgency to buy now. |
| **Discount code in the cart** | A code box in the order summary shows the savings and the new total. Only the owner's account can use the code, and expired codes are refused. | Seeing the savings before checkout nudges shoppers to complete the order, and the checks keep discounts under control. |
| **Interactive Bulldog** | The chat icon is a drawn bulldog that randomly winks, barks ("Woof! Woof!") or licks when clicked. | A playful, on-brand moment invites shoppers to chat. Chatting surfaces products, sizes and stock, which leads to sales. |
| **Mobile-friendly** | The banner, headline and chat all adapt to phone screens. | Most students shop on their phones, and a page that's easy to use there means fewer drop-offs. |

**Rule kept throughout:** prices, stock and discount codes always come from the server and database, never from the AI or the browser. That way shoppers can trust every number they see.
